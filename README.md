## 🏰 Hawthorne Castle Adventure

A choose-your-own-path text adventure. Reach the castle gates before midnight — or don't.

### Story
At the edge of a mysterious forest, a distant castle waits as the sun sets and a storm rolls in from the North. A chill is in the air, and time is short: you must reach the castle gates before midnight.

### Gameplay

**1. Enter the forest**
You find a lantern on the ground.
- **Pick it up** → continue deeper into the forest
- **Leave it** → ☠️ Death

**2. The fork in the path**
One path is overgrown with glowing eyes watching from the brush; the other is calm and well-kept.
- **Overgrown path** → ☠️ Death
- **Maintained path** → continue

**3. The rose bushes**
Near the castle gates, two rose bushes: red and black.
- **Red roses** → continue
- **Black roses** → wrong choice, but you continue
- **No roses** → wrong choice, but you continue

*(Note: this scene has 3 variants depending on the choice made here.)*

**4. The bridge troll**
A troll blocks the bridge and demands a gift before you may cross.
- **Give red roses** → allowed to cross, reach the castle doors
- **Give black roses** → ☠️ Death
- **Give nothing** → ☠️ Death

### Endings
- 🏰 **Victory** - you make it to the front gates
- ☠️ **Death** - you failed to pick up the lantern or failed to appease the troll
