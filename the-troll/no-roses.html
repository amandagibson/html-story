The Troll

You come across a bridge that leads to a clearing and suddenly, a menacing and growling troll appears and blocks your path. He shouts that he is the guardian of this bridge and in order to pass you must bestow a gift upon him but it must be the right gift.

The air is cold and the wind howls through the trees but you've almost made it.
You can see the gates of Hawthorne Castle just beyond.

Do you wish to:

  • Go back and pick the roses
  • Gift him nothing
  • Try to run past him
